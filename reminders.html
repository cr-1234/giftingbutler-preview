Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before your first reminder.

Is it really free?

Yes. Gift reminders are completely free, with no limits on how many you can set.

How far ahead will you remind me?

You decide. When you create a reminder, choose how many days before the occasion you'd like to hear from us — a week for planners, a day or two for the rest of us.

Can I turn reminders off?

Anytime. Delete a reminder in one click from your account, or unsubscribe directly from any reminder email. No questions, no hoops.

Do I need to keep the site open?

No. Email reminders arrive in your inbox on their own. Browser push notifications work even when the site is closed — you just allow them once.
